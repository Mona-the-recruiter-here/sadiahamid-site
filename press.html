Short bio

Sadia Hamid is a product and digital transformation leader with more than a decade of experience across telecoms, ecommerce and education technology. She is the founder of Skaadi Ltd, which trades as WizardTeacher, an AI exam-preparation platform. She is based in Sheffield.

Full bio

Sadia Hamid is a product and digital transformation leader and the founder of Skaadi Ltd, the company behind WizardTeacher, an AI exam-preparation platform covering IELTS, TOEFL, PTE, SAT and ACT. Over more than seven years at du in Dubai she directed digital transformation for a nine-million-customer base, re-platformed more than 10,000 products and shipped machine-learning personalisation into production. She later took AI products from idea to production at Keystone Education Group, a global education technology marketplace. Sadia holds an Executive MBA from Bayes Business School in London and is based in Sheffield.
