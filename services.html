Expertise

Where I add the most value

Product strategy and leadership

Vision, roadmap and prioritisation for a product or a portfolio, with commercial and ROI decisions built in. Available as interim, fractional or permanent leadership of a cross-functional team.

Digital transformation

Planning and running large re-platforming programmes, from legacy estates to modern platforms, with steering-level governance, risk management and vendor coordination.

Data migration and governance

Full-lifecycle migration across core systems: mapping, cleansing, automated ETL, cutover and UAT. Plus data contracts, quality frameworks and validation gates so data can be trusted.

AI product delivery

Finding the use cases that pay back, then shipping them: recommendation engines, retention models, assistants and AI content pipelines, with hands-on prototyping in Python and the Claude API.

Go-to-market and growth

Launch strategy, positioning and pricing, plus conversion-rate and search recovery for lead-driven marketplaces.

Founder and board advisory

An experienced view on product-market fit, build-versus-buy and where to invest next, for founders, leadership teams and boards.
