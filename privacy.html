Who is responsible

This website is run by Sadia Hamid, who is the data controller for any personal information described here. You can reach her at sadia@wizardteacher.com.

What this site collects

This site has no forms, no accounts, no analytics and no advertising. It sets no cookies. Fonts and files are served from the site itself, so your browser does not contact third-party font or tracking services when you visit.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address to keep the service secure. GitHub processes that data under its own privacy statement.

When you get in touch

If you email Sadia or message her on LinkedIn, she uses your name, contact details and message only to reply and to handle your request. She keeps that correspondence only as long as needed for that purpose, and does not sell or share it.

Links to other sites

Links to articles, podcasts, LinkedIn and WizardTeacher open those sites in a new tab. Their own privacy policies apply there.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see, correct or delete personal information held about you, or object to how it is used. Email the address above to make a request. If you are unhappy with the response, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.
