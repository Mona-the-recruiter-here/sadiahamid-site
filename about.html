About

A product leader who builds, not just directs

I sit where strategy, data and technology meet. My work has been to take complex organisations and make them run on better digital products.

My story

From telecom-scale change to my own AI company

My career spans telecoms, ecommerce and education technology. For more than seven years at du, one of the UAE’s leading telecom and digital services providers, I directed digital transformation for a nine-million-customer base, re-platformed more than 10,000 products and shipped machine-learning personalisation into production.

I went on to take AI products from idea to production at Keystone Education Group, a global education technology marketplace. Along the way I completed an Executive MBA at Bayes Business School in London, which widened my view of how innovation happens in markets from Silicon Valley to Nairobi and Vietnam.

In 2026 I founded Skaadi Ltd, the company behind WizardTeacher. It is an AI exam-preparation platform that is live and earning revenue, built from Sheffield.

I care about products that are commercially sound, well engineered, and simple to use.
