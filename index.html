My story

Why I went my own way

For years I led large programmes for other people. I was proud of that work, but every part of it followed someone else’s roadmap.

In 2026 I chose to put that experience behind a product of my own, and founded Skaadi Ltd, the company behind WizardTeacher.

I am not a traditional developer. What I bring is judgement: knowing what users need, and spotting an answer that sounds right but isn’t. That turned out to be exactly what building with AI demands.

I did it outside London, and I want others to see that serious companies can start anywhere.
