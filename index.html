AboutThe short version

I am a product leader who sits where strategy, data and technology meet. My work has been to take complex organisations and make them run on better digital products.

For more than seven years at du, one of the UAE's leading telecom and digital services providers, I played a central part in the company's digital transformation, from customer value management strategy to the data architecture and migrations that made it possible.

Since then I have led product work in education technology, and founded Skaadi Ltd, the company behind WizardTeacher, an AI exam-preparation platform that is live and earning revenue.

I care about products that are commercially sound, well engineered, and quietly elegant to use.
